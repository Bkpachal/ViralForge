ViralForge: Turn Proven Video Formats Into Original AI Videos

ViralForge is an AI-powered short-form video creation platform from Abhi Dwivedi and VineaSX Solutions. It is designed for affiliate marketers, product owners, content creators and small agencies that want to create promotional videos without filming themselves or editing every clip manually.

Its central idea is to remake a successful video's format rather than repost its original footage. The workflow combines TrendRadar, ViralDNA, SwapEngine and FreshHooks to analyze a format, build a new script around an offer and generate up to 25 variations in a batch.

This review covers the product's workflow, features, intended users, practical limitations, historical launch pricing and upgrade funnel. What's Inside ViralForge?

The core package combines three main parts of a short-form video marketing workflow:

1. Trend Research & ViralDNA

TrendRadar helps users find videos and advertisements gaining traction in a selected niche. ViralDNA then analyzes elements such as the hook, structure, pace, captions and visual style.

The goal is to understand the format that attracted attention and use that structure as inspiration for a new piece of content.

2. SwapEngine & AI Video Production

SwapEngine adapts the selected format to a user's offer, script, presenter, voice, language and call to action. FreshHooks and the Multiplier can produce up to 25 versions with different hooks, captions, hashtags and on-screen text.

The platform includes AI presenters, voices and B-roll. The vendor says users do not need to connect their own API keys or pay separate AI generation bills.

3. Scheduling, Bio-Link & Analytics

The Smart Scheduler organizes publishing to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, while the built-in bio-link page gives profile visitors a place to find promoted offers.

Analytics show video views and clicks, and Clone Again lets users build another batch around a format that performed well.

What Is ViralForge?

ViralForge is a browser-based AI video marketing application built around a 'remake, don't repost' workflow. A user selects a video from the trend feed or supplies a link to a TikTok, Reel, Short or advertisement. The platform analyzes its format and helps create a new video with a different script, AI presenter, voice, visuals and offer.

The intended marketing path is to publish short videos, direct viewers to a profile bio-link page and earn a commission if someone purchases through an affiliate link. The Offer Library helps users discover offers from JVZoo, ClickBank and WarriorPlus, but the review source notes that users still need to request and obtain affiliate approval through the relevant marketplace.

ViralForge provides content production and publishing tools; it does not provide guaranteed traffic or income. Users remain responsible for choosing suitable offers, checking generated claims, following platform policies and disclosing affiliate relationships.

How Does ViralForge Work?

The workflow moves from trend discovery to format analysis, offer selection, video generation, publishing and performance review.

1

Step 1: Find a Video That Is Gaining Attention

Use TrendRadar to explore rising videos and ads in a niche, or paste a link to a TikTok, Instagram Reel, YouTube Short or advertisement. The review describes TrendRadar as drawing on public resources such as TikTok Creative Center and Meta Ad Library.

2

Step 2: Analyze the Format With ViralDNA

ViralDNA examines the hook, structure, pace, captions and visual style. The interface is described as showing which elements the AI intends to retain and which it will change for the new video.

3

Step 3: Choose an Offer and Customize the Script

Select an offer from the Offer Library, which lists marketplaces including JVZoo, ClickBank and WarriorPlus, or use a link to your own product. SwapEngine adapts the script, presenter, voice, language and call to action. Users must separately obtain affiliate approval and their affiliate links from the relevant marketplace.

4

Step 4: Generate Video Variations

Use the Multiplier to render up to 25 versions in a batch on the front-end package. FreshHooks gives versions different openings, captions, hashtags, on-screen text and framing. The review says rendering takes place in the background and an email notification is sent when the batch is ready.

5

Step 5: Schedule or Download the Videos

The Smart Scheduler supports TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, with three posts per day described as the default schedule. Users can also download MP4 files. The review specifically notes that Facebook is handled through downloads rather than direct publishing from the scheduler.

6

Step 6: Review Analytics and Reuse a Strong Format

Review the available views and clicks data to compare creative variations. Clone Again can use a selected format as the starting point for another batch. Results still depend on the audience, offer, creative quality, publishing consistency and platform distribution.

What Makes the ViralForge Approach Different?

A generic AI video tool may ask a user to provide a topic and prompt, then leave the user to decide the hook, structure, pacing and promotional angle. ViralForge instead starts with a video format that the user wants to study.

With ViralForge, the user can analyze an existing format, identify its hook and pacing, then create a new script around a selected offer with a different presenter, voice and visuals. FreshHooks can create multiple opening variations for testing.

The advantage is a more guided production process, not a guarantee that the new video will become popular. The final message still needs to be relevant, accurate and appropriate for the intended audience.
